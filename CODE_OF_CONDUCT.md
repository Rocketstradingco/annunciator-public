# Code of conduct

Be kind and constructive. Assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people,
and help newcomers. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language
are not welcome in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation that
breaks these rules. To raise a concern privately, contact the maintainers
through the repository's security reporting channel (see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and say it is a conduct report.
